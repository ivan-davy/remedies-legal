# Medical Disclaimer

**Effective date:** 16 August 2026
**Last updated:** 26 October 2026


---


Remedies helps you keep track of the medicines, supplements, supplies and equipment you have at home. Please read this before you rely on it.

## Not a medical device

Remedies organises and reminds. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent anything, and no medicines regulator has reviewed or approved it.

## Not medical advice

Nothing in the app is medical or pharmaceutical advice. Ask a doctor or pharmacist about doses, interactions, side effects and whether a medicine suits you.

## Check what it fills in

Remedies shows what you typed in or scanned. From its own lists it suggests names, active substances and makers, and each suggestion fills in only its own field. It also offers labels you can add and shows a substance’s ATC code. None of this is checked against any official source. Register Fill copies a product from a public register of medicines, such as the FDA’s in the United States, and a register can be out of date or wrong too. So names, substances, strengths, doses and expiry dates can be wrong. Read the packaging or the leaflet before taking anything.

## Reminders can be late or missed

Reminders are ordinary phone notifications. iOS can delay or drop them, and none arrive while the phone is off. Never let Remedies be the only thing standing between you and a dose that matters.

## Not for emergencies

In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.
