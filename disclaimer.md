# Medical Disclaimer

**Effective date:** 16 August 2026
**Last updated:** 12 November 2026


---

Remedies keeps track of the medicines, supplements, supplies and equipment you have at home. Please read this before you rely on it.

## Not a medical device

Remedies organises your records and reminds you. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent anything, and no medicines regulator has reviewed or approved it.

## Not medical advice

Nothing in the app is medical or pharmaceutical advice. Ask a doctor or pharmacist about doses, interactions, side effects and whether a medicine suits you.

## Check what it fills in

Remedies shows what you typed in or scanned. From its own lists it suggests names, active substances and makers, and each suggestion fills in only its own field. It also offers labels you can add and shows a substance’s ATC code. None of these suggestions is checked against any official source. Register Fill copies a product from a public register of medicines, such as the FDA’s in the United States, and a register can be out of date or wrong too. Names, substances, strengths, doses and expiry dates can therefore be wrong. Read the packaging or the leaflet before taking anything.

## About reminders

Reminders are ordinary notifications. The operating system can delay or drop them, and none arrive while your device is off. Take particular care with time-critical doses.

## Not for emergencies

In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.
